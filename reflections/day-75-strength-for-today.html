Strength for Today

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Seventy-five days in, I still don’t have it all figured out — and that’s okay. Some mornings feel heavy before the day even starts, but I’ve learned I don’t have to carry it alone. God is with me, steadying me, giving me strength for just this one day.

“Fear not, for I am with you.”— Isaiah 41:10

If you’re struggling today, you don’t need strength for the whole road ahead — just for today. Take one step, ask for help, and trust that God will meet you right where you are. You’re not alone, and this day still matters.

— Steven

Need help now? In the U.S., call or text 988 any time for free, confidential crisis support, or call SAMHSA’s National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 for treatment referrals. You do not have to carry this alone.
