A Day the Lord Has Made

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

A birthday can make you look back at everything that went wrong — the years lost, the people hurt, the chances missed. I know those thoughts. But this verse turns my eyes to today. Today is a day God made, and I get to live it sober. I can’t change the past birthdays, but I can be present for this one, grateful for mercy I didn’t earn, and faithful with the day in front of me.

“This is the day the Lord has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it.”— Psalm 118:24 (NLT)

Consider: What is one thing about today — just today — that you can thank God for, even in the middle of rebuilding?

— Steven
