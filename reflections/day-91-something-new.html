November 19, 2026Sober Day 91

Something New

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

God spoke these words to people who were stuck in a place they never chose, a long way from home, with no road back that they could see. And He didn’t start by explaining the route. He started by saying: I’m already doing something new. I’ve already begun. Do you not see it?

I love that He asks, “Do you not see it?” — because most days, I don’t. I look at my life and see the same wilderness: the same questions about what comes next, the same waiting, the same one day at a time. But this verse says the new thing doesn’t start when I can see it. It starts while I’m still standing in the wilderness, still waiting, still praying. The pathway is being made even when all I can see is desert.

That’s what Day 91 feels like to me. Ninety days behind me, and the road ahead still mostly unknown. But I don’t have to see the whole road. I only have to trust the One who makes pathways where there aren’t any — and take the next step on the one in front of me.

“For I am about to do something new. See, I have already begun! Do you not see it? I will make a pathway through the wilderness. I will create rivers in the dry wasteland.”— Isaiah 43:19 (NLT)

Consider: Where in your life does it feel like wilderness right now — and what would change if you believed God had already begun something new there?

— Steven
