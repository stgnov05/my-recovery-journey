A New Life Has Begun

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

God doesn’t say, “Try harder to become a better version of your old self.” He says a new person. The old life is gone — not improved, not on probation, gone — and a new life has begun. That’s not a feeling to work up; that’s a fact to agree with.

That’s why the I am’s work. “I am worthy. I am free. I am enough.” They aren’t wishes or positive thinking. They’re a man repeating back what God already said about him, until his heart catches up with the truth.

“This means that anyone who belongs to Christ has become a new person. The old life is gone; a new life has begun!”— 2 Corinthians 5:17 (NLT)

Consider: Which of the seven I am’s is hardest for you to believe today — and what would change if you let God be right about it?

— Steven
