Not a Spirit of Fear

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Fear ran my life for years. Fear of the morning, fear of being found out, fear of trying to stop and failing again. Most of my drinking was just fear with a bottle in its hand.

Paul says that spirit doesn’t come from God. What God gives instead is power — His, not mine, which is good news, because mine ran out a long time ago. Love — for the people I’m rebuilding with, and slowly, for myself. And self-discipline — not the white-knuckle kind, but the quiet daily kind: pray, show up, tell the truth, don’t pick up the first drink, go to bed sober, do it again tomorrow.

Fear still knocks. It probably always will. But it doesn’t get to make my decisions anymore, because it was never the spirit I was given.

“For God has not given us a spirit of fear and timidity, but of power, love, and self-discipline.”— 2 Timothy 1:7 (NLT)

Consider: Where is fear making your decisions today — and what would change if you answered it with the power, love, and self-discipline God actually gave you?

— Steven
