November 12, 2026Sober Day 84

Trust Him With the Path

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Trust with all my heart — not with the part of my heart that has it figured out, and not after I’ve mapped the whole route. My own understanding has led me in circles for 34 years. It told me I could handle one more drink, that I could quit on my own, that waiting meant losing. It was wrong about all of it.

Seeking His will in all I do is smaller than it sounds. It’s not a grand plan. It’s the one step in front of me — this morning, this prayer, this honest choice — and then trusting Him to show the next one. He doesn’t show me the whole path at once. He shows me the path, one step at a time, and that’s enough, because the path is His to show and mine to walk.

“Trust in the LORD with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take.”— Proverbs 3:5-6 (NLT)

Consider: What is the one step in front of you today — and can you trust God with everything past it?

— Steven
