Hold On to Hope

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Hold tightly — that’s the instruction. Not “hope vaguely” or “wait and see.” Hold on, the way you’d hold a rope, the way you’d hold a hand in the dark. Some days my grip feels strong. Some days hope feels like the only thing holding on to me. Either way, the promise underneath the hope doesn’t move, because it doesn’t rest on my grip. It rests on God’s character — and He can be trusted to keep His promise.

Wavering isn’t failure, either. The verse assumes we’ll be tempted to waver; that’s why it tells us not to. On the days I waver, I borrow strength from the promise itself: God has never once dropped what He said He would hold.

“Let us hold tightly without wavering to the hope we affirm, for God can be trusted to keep his promise.”— Hebrews 10:23 (NLT)

Consider: What promise of God do you need to hold tightly to today — and what would change if you really believed He’ll keep it?

— Steven
