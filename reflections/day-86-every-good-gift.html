Every Good Gift

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Every good thing in my life today has the same return address. That’s what this verse tells me: the gifts come down from a Father who never changes and never casts a shifting shadow. My circumstances shift. My moods shift. He doesn’t.

Recovery is teaching me to trace gifts back to the Giver. Sobriety itself is a gift. So is a clear head, a second chance, a morning I actually want to be awake for. When I name them as gifts instead of accidents, gratitude stops being a mood and becomes a practice — and a man who practices gratitude is much harder to talk into throwing his life away.

“Whatever is good and perfect is a gift coming down to us from God our Father, who created all the lights in the heavens. He never changes or casts a shifting shadow.”— James 1:17 (NLT)

Consider: What good gift have you been treating as an accident — and what changes when you thank the Giver for it by name?

— Steven
