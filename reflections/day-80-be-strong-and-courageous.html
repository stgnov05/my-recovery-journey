Be Strong and Courageous

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

God didn’t tell Joshua to be strong because the road ahead was easy. He told him because it wasn’t. Courage isn’t the absence of fear — it’s taking the next step while the fear is still there. That’s what eighty days has taught me: I don’t have to feel ready for what’s ahead, or brave enough for the whole journey. I just have to be willing to take the step that’s in front of me, and trust that God goes with me when I take it.

“This is my command—be strong and courageous! Do not be afraid or discouraged. For the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”— Joshua 1:9 (NLT)

Consider: What is one step you’ve been putting off — and what would it look like to take just that one today?

— Steven
