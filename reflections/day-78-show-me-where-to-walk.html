Show Me Where to Walk

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Most of recovery doesn’t happen on the big days. It happens on ordinary ones — a quiet morning, a clear head, and a hundred small decisions nobody sees. David’s prayer wasn’t for a miracle; it was for direction — just for today. Show me where to walk. That’s a prayer I can pray on a day like this one: no milestone, no headline — just trust, and the next step in front of me.

“Let me hear of your unfailing love each morning, for I am trusting you. Show me where to walk, for I give myself to you.”— Psalm 143:8 (NLT)

Consider: What is the next right step in front of you today — not the whole road, just the next step?

— Steven
