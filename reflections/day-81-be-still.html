Be Still

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

God didn’t say “hurry up and know that I am God.” He says be still. Stillness isn’t doing nothing — it’s trusting that God is at work even when I can’t see it, and that my worth today isn’t measured by how much I got done. On the quiet days, the ones with no big victories to report, He is just as much God as He is on the mountain-top days. Maybe those are the days I need Him most.

“Be still, and know that I am God! I will be honored by every nation. I will be honored throughout the world.”— Psalm 46:10 (NLT)

Consider: What would it look like to let today be a still day — and to call that faithfulness instead of failure?

— Steven
