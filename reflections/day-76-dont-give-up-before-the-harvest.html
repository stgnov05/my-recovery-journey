Don’t Give Up Before the Harvest

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Some days in recovery feel like you’re doing the right things and nothing seems to be changing. You show up, you pray, you make the meeting, you choose not to isolate — and life still feels slow and hard. This verse reminds me that the work I’m doing today matters, even when I can’t see the results yet. A harvest doesn’t come the same day the seed is planted. My job today isn’t to fix everything. My job is to not give up, to keep doing the next right thing, and to trust God with the timing.

“So let’s not get tired of doing what is good. At just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don’t give up.”— Galatians 6:9 (NLT)

Consider: What is one good, small thing you can keep doing today — even if you can’t see it paying off yet?

— Steven
