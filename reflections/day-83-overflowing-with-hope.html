Overflowing with Hope

A daily reflection by Steven Gregory — in recovery since August 20, 2026.

Hope here isn’t something I have to manufacture. God is the source of it, and He fills me with it — joy and peace first, because I trust Him, and then hope overflows out of that. I don’t have to feel hopeful to start. I just have to trust Him with today, and the hope follows.

That’s the same shape as the small steps. I do the one thing in front of me, I trust God with the rest, and somewhere in the doing, hope shows up — not as a feeling I chased, but as an overflow I didn’t have to earn.

“I pray that God, the source of hope, will fill you completely with joy and peace because you trust in him. Then you will overflow with confident hope through the power of the Holy Spirit.”— Romans 15:13 (NLT)

Consider: What is the one small thing in front of you today — and can you trust God with everything past it?

— Steven
